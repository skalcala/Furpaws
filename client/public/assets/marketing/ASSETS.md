# FurPaws marketing assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for the local FurPaws website. These are illustrative promotional images, not photographs of the clinic’s actual personnel or premises. Original FurPaws branding remains unchanged.

## pets-hero.png

Transparent PNG. Final prompt:

> Create a photorealistic advertising website asset: a friendly happy golden retriever sitting closely beside a fluffy grey tabby cat, both facing camera, full chest and forepaws visible at the lower edge. Dog on left, cat on right lower foreground. Warm natural studio lighting, realistic detailed fur, affectionate healthy pets, high-end veterinary clinic campaign photography. Portrait/square composition sized for the right half of a website hero. Isolated clean cutout on a truly transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no text, no logos, no collars with tags, no extra animals. Preserve full ears and heads. This is a reusable image asset, not a screenshot or complete webpage.

## veterinary-care.png

Final prompt:

> Photorealistic editorial veterinary-care website photograph, square composition: a caring female veterinarian wearing a burgundy scrub top and a stethoscope gently checking a calm golden retriever on a clean exam table. Warm contemporary veterinary exam room, cream walls, soft daylight through a window, natural candid interaction, dog looking content, anatomically correct hands and instruments, no medical injury, premium authentic lifestyle advertising aesthetic. No text, no logos, no signage. Palette warm cream, muted burgundy and natural wood. This is illustrative promotional photography, not a portrait of any real clinic employee.

Both final files are saved in `client/public/assets/marketing/` and referenced locally by the site. The existing `imgPetPicture1.png` is reused from the supplied project as the supporting cat photograph.
